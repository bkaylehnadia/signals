ABOUT SIGNALS

What might we learn from the stories we watch? This fictional game explores social learning, representation, and repeated media messages through choices and conversations.

The brain shows accumulated experiences, not intelligence or a medical score. Scenery, expressions, and wiring are visual metaphors. People can question what they see and respond differently.

A complete three-chapter journey: childhood (ages 6–7), preteen life (age 11), and the teen years (ages 15–18), followed by a final choice and a personal ending.
